About GNOME Beta Store

GNOME Beta Store is a community-driven and unofficial initiative.

This project was born as a response to the current overload of the review process in the official GNOME extensions catalog. The bottleneck created by the limited number of human reviewers often results in waits of weeks or months for the publication of critical updates or new features.

Our mission is to provide an alternative, automated, and secure channel that allows developers to publish their work immediately after passing an automated audit based on AI and malware analysis.

This platform is not affiliated with the GNOME Foundation. Use extensions at your own risk.

Developers Guide

Publishing on GNOME Beta Store is a 100% GitHub-based process, with no need for external accounts.

  1. Go to our storage repository on GitHub.
  2. Open a new Issue using the "Upload / Update Extension" template.
  3. Fill out the form: UUID, Name, Description, and attach your ZIP file, icon, and screenshots.
  4. Our bot will run an automated audit:
    • Code Analysis (AI): Checks for GJS compatibility and best practices.
    • Security: Full analysis on VirusTotal.
  5. If the audit is successful, your extension will be automatically published on this website within minutes.
